How it works

Open a board

Open the app in your browser, or launch the add-on from the Google Meet sidebar.

Invite your team

Choose Copy invite link in Board options and send it to your team. In Meet, use the invite control.

Pick a topic

Vote on the topics, then start a timed discussion. Copy the board as Markdown when you’re done.

Your data

Your board is encrypted in your browser and synced live with your group. It isn’t saved after everyone leaves, so copy it as Markdown if you want to keep it. Read the privacy policy.
