In your browser

Open app.start.coffee in your desktop or mobile browser. No installation or Google sign-in is required. Add topics, upvote priorities, and start a timed discussion.

To collaborate, open Board options and choose Copy invite link, then share the link with your team. Choose Copy as markdown from the same menu to export your board. Boards are not saved after everyone leaves. Anyone with the invite link can join, so share it only with people you want on the board.

Google Meet installation

Install Meet for Coffee from the Google Workspace Marketplace listing. If your organization manages add-on installation, ask your Google Workspace administrator to approve Meet for Coffee.

Using the Meet add-on

Open Meet for Coffee from the Google Meet sidebar. Choose Invite your team to start a shared board, or join the activity another participant has started. Then add topics and vote on what to discuss. The app connects automatically using a guest session.

Common issues

  • Add-on does not appear: confirm the add-on is installed and enabled for your Google Workspace account.
  • Board will not load: check your connection. If it still fails, contact support with the error message. Do not include your full board invite link.
  • Board is empty after everyone left: boards are not saved. Use Copy as markdown before leaving if you need a record.
  • Organization administrator blocks installation: ask your administrator to approve the Marketplace app.

Data deletion

Email support@bif.dev to request deletion of information retained about you, such as support correspondence. Board history is not saved by the service. Clear this app’s browser storage to remove locally saved display names and guest sessions.
